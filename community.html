Being a good neighbor

A few friendly reminders from the association's Rules and Regulations. Owners will find the full summary on the Owners page, and guests on the Guests page.

Pool & quiet

The pool is open 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. Children under 10 need an adult present, and everyone swims at their own risk. The hot tub is not for children under 8, and children must be accompanied by an adult. Please keep noise, music and television low enough not to disturb your neighbors.

Pets

Pets are not allowed at the pool or on the dock. Only owners may keep a pet (one per unit, up to 30 lbs). Renters, guests and visitors may not have pets on the property. Details are on the Owners page.

Shared spaces

Please keep halls, stairs and sidewalks clear and take your belongings from shared areas. Food and drink outside a unit are for designated areas. [where the designated areas are - to confirm]

Community grills

Please be a good steward of the community grills. If there is no propane left, notify Henry or Maria (CAM). Please clean the grill after each use and put the cover back on every time.
