How it works

Every vendor who works in a unit at The Palms is checked once, earns a verified badge, and can then be hired by any owner without starting over. Most checks run automatically, so approvals are fast.

Coming soon. This page is a preview of the planned vendor program. The forms are not live yet. [Phase 1 Google Forms - links to come]

Three risk tiers

Tier 1: Low risk

Cleaners, furniture delivery, pet sitters, property-check services, interior designers (no installation).

Approved automatically when all checks pass.

Tier 2: Licensed trades

Plumbers, electricians, HVAC, appliance repair, painters, flooring, pest control, locksmiths, non-structural handyman work.

Approved automatically when every check is green. Anything missing goes to the CAM.

Tier 3: High risk / permitted work

General contractors, roofing, windows, doors and hurricane shutters, structural, concrete, demolition, dock and marine work.

Always reviewed by the community association manager (CAM) with a one-click approve or reject.

For owners

  1. Search the directory by name, trade or license number. A verified card means you can go straight to step 4.
  2. Not listed? Tap "Invite my vendor." Enter the company, contact, trade and your unit. It takes about 30 seconds, and you never handle their paperwork.
  3. Your vendor gets an email link and completes a one-time check-in. You see the status: Invited, Submitted, Verified.
  4. File a work notice with your unit, dates, scope and access details, and sign the owner acknowledgment.
  5. The office is notified of expected vendors for the day.

For vendors

  1. Open the emailed link and enter your license number. Public license records are checked automatically.
  2. Upload your certificate of insurance and the additional-insured endorsement.
  3. Upload workers' comp proof or your valid state exemption.
  4. Sign the vendor rules (hours, parking, elevator pads, debris removal, sign-in).
  5. Get your verified badge. You won't need to re-submit for other owners, only renewals before documents expire.

Vendor requirements

  • Licensed: active Florida DBPR license that matches the trade.
  • Local credentials for permitted work: Monroe County Certificate of Competency plus Village of Islamorada contractor registration.
  • Bonded and insured.
  • General liability: at least $1 million per occurrence ($2 million aggregate) for licensed trades. Higher limits for Tier 3.
  • Workers' compensation policy, or a valid Florida exemption.
  • Association named as additional insured by endorsement. Being listed only as "certificate holder" is not enough.
  • Active business registration and local business tax receipt.

Limits and tiers are a draft recommendation, pending review by the association's attorney and insurance agent.

Owner reviews

Planned owners-only system, not live yet. Owners who hired a vendor can rate them from 1 to 5 stars. To keep ratings fair:

  • One vote per unit, per vendor. Co-owners of a unit share one rating.
  • Verified jobs only. You can rate a vendor only after a completed work notice for that vendor at your unit.
  • Change it, don't stack it. You can update your own rating later, but you can't add a second one.
  • Owners only. Ratings and comments will be in the owners-only area, not on this public site.
  • Optional short comment. The CAM removes only personal attacks or private information, never an honest negative opinion.
  • No gaming. Vendors can't rate themselves or pay to change or remove reviews.
  • Minimum of 3 reviews before a vendor's average is shown.
  • Conflicts of interest. Anyone rating a business they have a personal or financial tie to (for example, a relative's company) must disclose it; such ratings are flagged and may be excluded.
