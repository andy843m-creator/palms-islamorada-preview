Owner rules at a glance

A friendly summary of the association's Rules and Regulations (Schedule A). It is not a substitute for the full document, which is part of the governing documents. If the rules ever conflict with the governing documents, the order of authority is the Declaration, then the Articles of Incorporation, then the By-Laws, then the Rules and Regulations. The Board of Directors may amend the rules from time to time.

Guests & security

  • Register each guest in writing, in advance, with the manager's office. Include the guest's name, address and car license tag number, if any.
  • Management keeps a roster of owners, their other addresses and phone numbers.
  • No one is admitted to an apartment without the owner's written authorization.
  • No solicitors in the building.
  • Report any suspicious person or incident to the manager right away.
  • Close and lock all windows and doors when you leave.

Moving in or out

  • The manager assigns moving times.
  • Moves are limited to 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday through Friday.
  • Owners are responsible for any damage caused by moving their furniture and property.

Hurricane season

  • If you will be away during hurricane season, designate a responsible firm or person (if not the Association) to care for your unit if it is damaged.
  • Give the Association that firm or person's name.
  • That firm or person must get clearance from the Association before installing or removing hurricane shutters.

Outside your unit

  • No signs, ads or notices visible from the common areas, including for-sale and for-rent signs.
  • No awnings, canopies, shutters or other projections on the outside walls or roof.
  • The outside look of balconies and terraces may not be changed.
  • Nothing may be placed on, or hung from, balconies, railings, windows, sills or doors. This includes laundry, linens and rugs.
  • Curtains and drapes facing outside are subject to Board approval.
  • Antennas or aerials on the roof or outside walls need prior Board consent, or the Board may remove them at the owner's cost.

Locks, repairs & installations

  • The Association may keep a pass-key to all units.
  • Changing or adding a lock needs the Board's written consent, and you give the Association an extra key.
  • Plumbing, wiring or air-conditioning installations need the Board's written approval.
  • Keep your unit and its fixtures in good repair, and pay utilities that are separately metered.
  • Keep flammable or explosive substances to normal household use.

Pets

  • Owners only: one pet per owner's apartment, up to 30 lbs. Renters are not allowed to have pets.
  • The pet may not be replaced with another pet.
  • Carry the pet out of the building, then leash it. Pets are not allowed on walkways, elevators, entrances or common areas.
  • Walk pets off the property or in assigned areas, never near the building, walkways or gardens.
  • No pets at the pool or on the dock. Renters, guests and visitors may not bring pets onto the property.
  • Bag pet waste tightly and use the provided trash containers, never the chutes.
  • The owner is responsible for any damage the pet causes. The Board may revoke the license if a pet is a nuisance.

Trash, parking & common areas

  • Wrap trash tightly in bags. Trash chutes may be used only 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. No lit cigarette butts or ashes.
  • Large boxes go neatly along the wall in the disposal rooms.
  • Keep halls, stairs, entrances and sidewalks clear. Store your belongings inside your unit.
  • Parking areas are for cars, delivery vehicles and bicycles. Boats, trailers, trucks, campers and commercial vehicles are not allowed except in designated areas, or with Association approval for boats and trailers.
  • A vehicle that cannot run on its own may not stay more than 24 hours. No vehicle repairs on the property.
  • Never block access to another owner's unit.
  • Food and drink outside a unit only in designated areas.
  • Community grills: please be a good steward. If there is no propane left, notify Henry or Maria (CAM). Clean the grill after each use and put the cover back on every time.

Pool & noise

  • The pool is open 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. Swim at your own risk.
  • Children under 10 must have an adult present.
  • Hot tub: no children under 8. Children must be accompanied by an adult.
  • Please avoid noises that disturb neighbors, and keep music, television and radio at a considerate level. Radio or television installations may not interfere with a neighbor's reception.

Office hours

  • Association business is handled in the office Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., except for emergencies.
  • Please do not send Association employees on personal errands. The Board of Directors directs and supervises them.

Full text: Schedule A, Rules and Regulations of The Palms of Islamorada, A Condominium. [link to full Rules and Regulations - to confirm]
